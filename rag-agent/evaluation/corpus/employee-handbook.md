# Employee Handbook

## Parental leave eligibility

Eligibility requirements include twelve months of continuous employment before an employee is eligible for parental leave. Full-time status on the date of the message is also required. Contractors are outside this benefit. The message must name the expected start date and the caregiver arrangement.

## Parental leave duration

Eligible employees receive sixteen weeks of paid parental leave. Those sixteen weeks may be taken continuously. The absence should start within half a year of the birth or placement. Unused weeks are not paid out at the end of the year.

## Parental leave approval

Manager approval for parental leave takes ten business days. Human resources confirms the decision in writing. An incomplete packet pauses that clock until the missing page arrives. The manager cannot shorten the written record once it is sent.
