# Refund Policy

## Refund window

The refund period is 30 days. Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date. A shopper whose message arrives after the window is declined. Include the order number and the original receipt with the message.

The desk reviews complete messages in the order they arrive. Items must be unused aside from ordinary inspection. A photo is required when the goods arrived damaged. The original payment method receives the money after the message is accepted. Payment timing is printed in the terms of service rather than in this window section.

The refund request window is 30 days. The product refund policy gives customers 30 days to request a refund, which differs from a cancellation.

## Digital goods

Opened digital downloads are not refundable once the download link has been used. An unused download license can still be returned inside the window above. Activation of a license key counts as use. Played streaming access counts as use.

## Exclusions

Shipping fees are kept when a purchase is sent back. Personalized and monogrammed goods are excluded. Gift cards are excluded. A subscription is not handled in this document.
